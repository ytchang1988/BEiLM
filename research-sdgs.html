Focus · Sustainable Development Goals

SDGs Campus Twin.

A digital twin of the campus, structured around the UN Sustainable Development Goals — turning daily operations into measurable progress.

Research Journey

From past to present.

  1. 2021 – 2022

    Campus data

    Predicting a university library’s electricity use from occupant Wi-Fi data, making shared bikes more useful, and NTU case studies on energy-efficiency rating information, solar parking layouts, and rooftop solar potential from satellite imagery — the groundwork for an information integration platform for smart decisions on campus sustainability.

  2. 2023 – 2024

    A campus digital twin

    A digital twin platform for NTU built on 3D building models, BIM, and IoT sensors — first for a climate-resilient campus, then for a net-zero, circular, and WELL campus — with point-cloud rooftop analysis and a review of how people interact with digital twins in building operations.

  3. 2025 – now

    Keeping the twin alive

    Streamlining sensor registration in BIM-based twins, a platform that visualizes campus sustainability, guidance on university sustainability rankings, Scan-to-BIM for energy retrofit, and workflows that keep asset, sensor, and meter records linked as BIM models change.
