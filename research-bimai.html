Focus · Building Information Modeling × AI

BIM-AI Project Assistant.

An AI assistant for BIM-based projects — supporting design checking, generative design, cost estimation, site inspection, and risk analysis across the project lifecycle.

Research Journey

From past to present.

  1. 2016 – 2021

    BIM foundations

    Mapping global BIM research trends and BIM education, applying BIM to design warning analysis for a major airport terminal, and later combining BIM with IoT in a dynamic indoor evacuation guidance system.

  2. 2023 – 2024

    Learning and decision factors

    A simulation game, “To BIM or Not to BIM”, that teaches AEC students how project delivery works; fuzzy cognitive maps of how BIM performance factors interact; and a first step into AI with neural networks and BIM for material cost estimation.

  3. 2025 – now

    AI-assisted projects

    Generative and automated design workflows for housing and public housing, BIM with AR and visual SLAM for site inspection, and LLM-based multi-agent simulation of construction project risks.
