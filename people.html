Director
Lab Director · Principal Investigator

Yun-Tsui Chang

張芸翠

Assistant Professor, College of Architecture, Feng Chia University
Founder & Director, BEiLM Research Lab

Architect, urban planner, and computational design researcher working at the intersection of building informatics, sustainability, and lifecycle thinking. Trained across architecture (NCKU), urban planning (UCL Bartlett), and civil engineering (NTU), with a decade of practice in green building certification and sustainable design. The lab she founded — BEiLM — investigates how informatics, intelligence, industrialization, and integration transform the way we design, operate, and retire the built environment.

01 / Research Expertise

Four research areas, one connected practice.

01

Design Theory & Computational Design

Generative methods, parametric modeling, and the computational logic underlying contemporary architectural design.

02

Urban Communities & Environmental Planning

Bridging architecture and urbanism — community-scale planning, sustainability frameworks, and the social dimensions of the built environment.

03

Building Construction & Construction Management

BIM, modular construction, and the project-delivery mechanisms that turn designs into buildings — with circular economy principles woven throughout.

04

Building Planning & Environmental Control

Building performance, energy modeling, digital twins for facility management, and the long operational life where most environmental impact accumulates.
