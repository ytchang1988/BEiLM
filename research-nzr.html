Focus · Net-Zero Resilient

NZR City Platform.

A city-scale platform for tracking, simulating, and accelerating the path to a net-zero, resilient built environment.

Research Journey

From past to present.

  1. 2020 – 2022

    Platforms for sustainable cities

    SimuCity and a decision support platform for a sustainable Taipei, a review of urban simulation platforms, BIM and GIS simulation of the energy and material circularity of Taipei’s building stock, transdisciplinary teaching on smart cities and digital democracy in the Guandu Plain, and co-created guidelines for climate-proof, net-zero communities and cities — including net-zero, circular housing.

  2. 2023 – 2025

    Building stocks and co-creation

    Building archetypes and parametric models for estimating the material use and environmental impacts of urban building stocks, automated window-to-wall ratio extraction, the impacts of science parks on their surroundings — and how circular social housing could ease them — and hackathons — CO2Uptake and HackYourDistrict — as models for co-creating a net-zero future.

  3. 2026 – now

    Resilient spatial decisions

    System dynamics and GIS for sustainable, resilient planning; citizen science and generative visioning for children’s independent mobility; demonstration sites for Taiwan’s sustainable spatial planning; and #HackYourDistrict 2026 on AI for liveable neighbourhoods.
